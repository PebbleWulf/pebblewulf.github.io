Boomcoin prize odds

Boomcoin has four mini games, played with tickets you win on the coin pusher. Tickets can't be bought, and every prize is in-game coins or gems: nothing pays out real money. Here is the chance of every prize.

Which game you get

A mini game costs three tickets. The game is picked at random, but never the same one twice in a row, so each of the other three has a 1 in 3 chance.

Pop & Match

Pop balloons to reveal prizes. Find three of the same prize and you win it. If six pops find no three of a kind, you get the consolation prize.
