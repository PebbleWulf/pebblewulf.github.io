Privacy Policy

Applies to all games published by Pebblewulff on Google Play.
Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pebblewulff ("we", "us") is an independent game developer based in Australia. This policy explains what information is collected when you play our games, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. We cannot see this data, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Our games show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic data such as crash information

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings or privacy options.

Your choices

  • Limit personalised ads: on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above, we do not share data with third parties.

Security

Data sent by AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
